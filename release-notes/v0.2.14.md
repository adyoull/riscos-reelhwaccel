Hardware video for RISC OS on the Raspberry Pi: H.264 decoding and now **encoding** on the VideoCore, and HEVC decoding on the Pi 4's HEVC block, for FFmpeg and Reel.

New since v0.2.8: an H.264 encoder (`h264_encvchiq`), Pi-confirmed and already in riscos-ffmpeg's riscos21, plus the fixes from a code audit and faster HEVC paths.

To watch or convert video with it, use Reel and FFmpeg from [riscos-ffmpeg](https://github.com/adyoull/riscos-ffmpeg) (riscos21 and later): they are built with this devkit.

## H.264 encoding: h264_encvchiq

    ffmpeg -c:v h264_vchiq -i in.mp4 -c:v h264_encvchiq -profile:v main -b:v 5M -c:a aac out.mp4

- **Input and output:** YUV420P in, even sizes up to 1920x1088 (1080p needs `gpu_mem=128`). H.264 Baseline, Main or High out, with no B frames.
- **Headers:** the SPS and PPS are the extradata for MP4 and Matroska, and come in front of the first packet otherwise. `repeat_headers` puts them in front of every keyframe.
- **Settings:** bit rate, keyframe interval, profile (by name or number), level, quantiser limits, and keyframes on request.
- **Speed (Pi 4):** 1080p30 encodes at about 72 frames a second and 720p at about 155. Every stream decoded back close to what was sent, and plays in FFmpeg's software decoder.
- **Converting** (riscos-ffmpeg's Convert window, a 1280x544 trailer with AAC):

  | How | Speed |
  |---|---|
  | `h264_vchiq` decoding + `h264_encvchiq` encoding | 3.4x real time |
  | ARM decoding + `h264_encvchiq` | 2.2x real time |
  | libx264 veryfast | 0.66x real time |

- **Constant bit rate:** the Pi 4's firmware doesn't offer it. `rc_constant` gives variable, which holds the asked rate on real content.

## Also since v0.2.8

- **Audit fixes (devkit 0.2.9):**
  - a use-after-free when frames outlive the HEVC decoder;
  - a DPB overrun on damaged streams;
  - black pixels in the brightest areas of halved 10-bit 4K;
  - picture size changes part way;
  - pictures refused in pipelined mode.
- **`hevc_hwdec` honours `skip_frame`**, so a player that has fallen behind can drop non-reference pictures at the block (devkit 0.2.11).
- **HEVC conversions:** checked and timed into each kind of memory, the video overlay included. It is as fast as cached memory for the halved conversion (hevcdec 0.1.12).

## What it does on a Raspberry Pi 4 (decoding)

| | Pictures a second | ARM time a picture |
|---|---|---|
| H.264 1080p (`h264_vchiq`, zero copy) | 85.7 | 0.13 ms |
| HEVC 1080p 8-bit (`hevc_hwdec`, pipelined) | 353 | 4.0 ms converting (2.0 halved) |
| HEVC 4K 10-bit | 79 | 18.0 ms to 8-bit, 10.0 ms halved |

## Known

- **Pi 4 only so far.** vcdec and vcenc should work on a Pi 2 and 3 but haven't been tried there.
- **Upgrading:** the encoder was called `h264_vchiq` in devkits 0.2.12 and 0.2.13. It is `h264_encvchiq` from 0.2.14.
- **Requirements:** RISC OS 5 with the VCHIQ module (in the Pi ROMs) for H.264.
