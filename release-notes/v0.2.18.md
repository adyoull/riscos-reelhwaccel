Hardware video for RISC OS on the Raspberry Pi: H.264 decoding and encoding on the VideoCore, and HEVC decoding on the Pi 4's HEVC block, for FFmpeg and Reel.

New since v0.2.17: the VideoCore now writes decoded H.264 pictures straight into RISC OS memory, as Linux does. Nothing is copied over VCHIQ, and up to three 1080p decoders work at once.

## Direct output

Until now, the VideoCore decoded each picture into its own memory (gpu_mem) and then sent it to RISC OS by bulk transfer, 3 MB a picture at 1080p.

Now vcdec shares its picture buffers with the VideoCore through the firmware's shared-memory service (the one Linux's vc-sm-cma driver uses, which RISC OS had no client for), and sets MMAL's zero copy on the decoder's output. The decoder writes each picture straight into vcdec's buffer:

- **Nothing is transferred:** the transfers cost the ARM nothing.
- **Less gpu_mem used:** the firmware keeps no copy of the output buffers.
- **Nothing to change in a program:** it's on by default. If the service isn't there, or refuses, vcdec uses bulk transfers as before. `VCDEC_NO_DIRECT` turns it off.
- **One connection per program:** a program's decoders share one connection to the service. The firmware refused a third.

Measured on a Pi 4 (VCDecTest 0.3.7, 1080p, every picture of three clips right):

| | Direct | Bulk transfers |
|---|---|---|
| Pictures copied out | 82.4 a second | 79.7 a second |
| Pictures held, read once | 85.7 a second | 81.0 a second |

Decoding itself is the limit, so the speed gain is small. The real gain is memory.

## How many decoders at once (Pi 4, gpu_mem=128)

| Clip | Decoders at once | v0.2.17 | v0.2.18 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1080p | 1 | Works | Works, 60 pictures a second |
| 1080p | 2 | The second waits for the first | Both decode together |
| 1080p | 3 | — | All three decode together, 60 a second in all |
| 360p | 2, 3, 4 | Work | Work, 375 to 387 a second in all |

Four 1080p decoders weren't tried, and with six the VideoCore stopped answering (v0.2.15). **Programs can keep up to three 1080p hardware decoders open**, fewer beside an encoder. A decoder with no room fails after 5 s with "the VideoCore has no memory left for it", and FFmpeg falls back to the ARM.

## Fixed

- **A missing last picture.** With direct output, the VideoCore can hand back the end of the stream before the last picture: Multi with 3 and 4 decoders ended one short. vcdec now takes a picture that comes after the end first, and waits up to 0.2 s at the end while pictures are still owed.

## Upgrading

- `vcdec_stats` is bigger (`direct_on`, `direct`, `after_eos`), so rebuild anything that uses it against the new headers.
- **Still to do in a program:** a program ended by Escape, or killed (as riscos-ffmpeg's Convert window's Stop does), runs no exit handlers. Its decoders and encoders stay in the VideoCore until a restart. Call `vcdec_close_all()`, `vcenc_close_all()` and `hevcdec_close_all()` from your own signal handler, and stop a child program with Escape rather than killing it.
